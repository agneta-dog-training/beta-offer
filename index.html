Hi, I'm Agneta.

I'm a dog trainer and behaviour specialist, and I'm testing a new package structure. I've got a few free client spots in exchange for honest feedback (and a testimonial if you end up loving it!)

A bit about how I work: if you've got a dog who's struggling, you've probably already been told to be firmer, or tire her out more, or that she'll grow out of it. You've tried the Internet's advice, maybe a trainer or two (or three). Some of it helped a little, some did nothing, some made things worse. The problem is generic advice with not enough guidance or support along the way.

That's the part I'm into. I work with behaviour issues that make ordinary days harder than they need to be: barking, growling, fear and anxiety, over-excitement, leash pulling, the recall that only works in the kitchen, the vet and grooming visits that go sideways. My job isn't to "make" your dog behave, it's to figure out what's actually driving the behaviour and build solutions that fit your real dog and your real life.

I don't use any methods that rely on pain, fear, or intimidation, and I always refer out if I'm not confident I can help. So if you're unsure, just reach out and I'll tell you honestly.

  • What to expect — Sessions are similar to in-person. I coach you through the training plan and can demo using video, my own dog, or a stuffed animal as needed.
